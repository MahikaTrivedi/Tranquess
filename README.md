# 💙 Emotional Wellness Tracker

A browser-based emotional wellness application that helps users track their mood, journal daily, vent safely, and receive AI-driven calming support. Built entirely on the frontend, the app predicts emotional spirals and offers real-time guidance to manage stress and anxiety without requiring any backend integration.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🌤️ Mood Check-Ins**  
  Log your emotions daily with emojis, colors, and personalized tags.

- **📓 Journaling System**  
  Reflect on your day in a secure, local-only space.

- **🗣️ Vent Room**  
  Safely express emotions without judgment — your thoughts stay private.

- **💨 Calming Exercises**  
  Includes breathing animations, grounding techniques, and voice-guided relaxation using Web Speech API.

- **🔮 Panic Prediction Engine**  
  AI-powered detection of emotional patterns to suggest early interventions.

- **📊 Emotional Analytics**  
  View mood trends, journaling streaks, and emotion heatmaps over time.

- **🌙 Night Mode & Themes**  
  Switch between calming themes like Ocean, Aurora, and Candlelight for a personalized vibe.

- **🧘‍♀️ "Undo the Spiral" Button**  
  One-tap grounding when you're overwhelmed — instant calming exercises appear.

- **🎭 Emotional Avatar**  
  Your mood visualized as a character that reacts to your emotional state.
